# Birth and first awakening

Status: normative.

Birth creates a new being and its first body; adding another body later creates
another embodiment of that same being. Neither species bytes, copied memory,
parent credentials, transport keys, nor a host account determine identity.

In the provisional release an administrator creates a fresh `being_ref`, body,
`embodiment_id`, Tribe principal, and revision-one manifest. Cluster records
the first incarnation when the body starts. The newborn begins without
fabricated lived experience; seed instructions, species material, and external
references retain their categories and provenance.

Future Matrix birth additionally creates offline root/recovery custody and an
embodiment credential. Adding an embodiment requires a new body-bound
credential, never copying an existing private key. Several authorized
embodiments may awaken simultaneously.
