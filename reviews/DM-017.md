## Normative design

- **Closed categories:** only `personal-experience`, `personal-insight`, and
  `personal-skill` belong to `/me.memory`. `tribal-knowledge`,
  `external-reference`, `species-inheritance`, and `incarnation-state` retain
  distinct authorities.
- **Mechanical authorship boundary:** personal records are DM-011 events signed
  by the same `me_id`. Imported peer events, source publications, tribal rows,
  species releases, model output, snapshots, and projection rows cannot enter
  a personal category directly.
- **Correction lanes:** each memory has one predecessor-linked lane. Correction
  and retraction append immutable events; siblings quarantine and occupied
  positions never reopen.
- **Attributed learning:** external promotion creates only an
  `external-reference`. Tribal/source/species/handoff learning requires a
  later local insight or skill record citing exact evidence and containing only
  `/me`'s new statement.
- **Experience/body binding:** personal experience binds the exact DM-010
  session/body and remains admissible offline only beneath the exact event
  cutoff committed at park/wake.
- **Librarian boundary:** a model/worker may propose content. Deterministic
  policy/review plus `/me`'s accepted operational credential alone author the
  canonical record.
- **Ephemeral incarnation:** NOW, prompts, harness sessions, queues, caches,
  process state, and body status are scoped to one presence session. A fresh
  wake creates fresh incarnation state.
- **Projection boundary:** HMK, Wiki/compaii-state, indexes, embeddings,
  summaries, native harness memory, volumes, and snapshots are rebuildable
  projections or external sources. Database copying is never synchronization.
- **Park/wake boundary:** a curated signed handoff may cite memory heads and
  cursors, but the canonical ledger cutoff/checkpoint and DM-010 lease receipt
  remain authoritative. A projection or cluster rollback never lowers an
  accepted high-water.
- **Truth limitation:** signatures prove authorship and structured provenance,
  not factual truth or sincerity. Implementations nevertheless reject every
  mechanical re-attribution path under their control.

## Direct adversarial findings resolved

The implementation pass explicitly closed these likely category-confusion
failures before requesting review:

- **promotion laundering:** DM-015's previously open-ended
  `target_memory_category` now permits exactly `external-reference`; a separate
  local event is required for learning;
- **peer convergence laundering:** another `/me`'s valid personal event remains
  peer evidence after `/we.sync`; shared name/species or majority cannot make it
  local autobiography;
- **species-skill confusion:** inherited code/capability remains species state;
  a learned skill requires same-identity practice/adaptation evidence;
- **incarnation continuity confusion:** a copied NOW/session/snapshot cannot
  continue a presence session or mint a personal event; wake starts fresh state
  from a verified canonical cutoff;
- **handoff self-reference:** the handoff payload does not embed the hash of
  its own eventual cutoff. It is the credential's final event and the later
  checkpoint/lease names that enclosing event, avoiding an impossible
  self-referential event ID;
- **projection authority escalation:** HMK/database rows without source events
  are discarded or quarantined and cannot append canonical state;
- **correction overwrite/fork:** successor lanes retain original bytes and
  quarantine siblings rather than choosing by arrival or content;
- **worker authorship:** DeepSeek/librarian output is a cited proposal, not a
  personal event until the deterministic path and `/me` credential authorize
  it;
- **revocation erasure:** tribal/source revocation deactivates access/projection
  without rewriting lived interaction history or evidence references; and
- **cluster authority collision:** deployment checkpoints/fences may orchestrate
  park/wake but cannot sign memory, invent event cutoffs, issue presence leases,
  or regress identity/event high-waters.
