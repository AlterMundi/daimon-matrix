Kimi Code CLI (`kimi-code/k3-256k`) pass dispatched through ia-bridge

## Kimi disposition

Kimi's verdict was **READY WITH NOTES**: all acceptance criteria met, no
weakening of the DM-010 `/we` predicate, and one genuine internal
contradiction plus several underspecified points, all fixable in wording.

All eight findings were integrated into the publication candidate:

1. **(HIGH) Reply addressing vs. `/me` locality.** Section 7 now addresses a
   reply to the concrete sender identity carried by the original message
   (`me_id`, plus originating `incarnation_id` for `/we`-resolved messages),
   routed through published route references; `/me` stays local-only, with a
   new Section 12 negative row rejecting remote-`/me` reply addressing.
2. **(MEDIUM-HIGH) `/everyone` vs. the categorical adapter-signal rule.**
   Section 4 now names the only two sanctioned exceptions (`/here` embodiment
   evidence, `/everyone` reachability) where the rule is stated, and Section
   5.4 forbids new reachability-based scopes without a protocol version
   change.
3. **(MEDIUM-HIGH) `accepted` vs. terminal receipts.** Section 6.3 now
   defines `accepted` as a non-terminal pending state and exactly five
   terminal receipts; the timeout classification rule is made exact
   (deadline-without-ack = `failed:transport`, locally known TTL/evidence
   expiry = `expired`); Section 10 and Section 12 updated to match.
4. **(MEDIUM) `/all` coordinator evidence.** Section 4.8 now requires cluster
   membership evidence to be embodiment or presence evidence and forbids
   hub/broker/adapter rosters from defining `/all`; Section 12 row added.
5. **(MEDIUM-LOW) Dedup key typing.** Section 6.2 types the recipient
   component per scope class (`incarnation_id` for `/we`, relationship
   principal identifier for relationship scopes) and excludes route
   references and certificate generations from the key; Section 12 row added.
6. **(LOW) `/source` evidence-first wording.** Section 4.4 reworded: signed
   claims are the evidence; policy classifies ingested claims as admitted,
   quarantined, or rejected; only admitted claims resolve.
7. **(LOW) Receipt edge cases.** Route reference and adapter fields are now
   specified as present when a route was selected and null otherwise;
   timeout classification fixed (finding 3).
8. **(EDITORIAL) Notes.** "adaptor" typo unified; `.diff` "corresponding
   state" typed per scope; `/me.tell` documented as producing a local ledger
   record rather than a receipt vector; `/we` self-inclusion now states it
   is evaluated against the same DM-010 Section 10 predicate.

After integration, the specification carries 36 negative-scenario rows in
Section 12. No finding required redesign; none relaxed DM-010.
