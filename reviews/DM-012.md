DM-012 was re-reviewed after DM-010 and DM-011 corrected the identity model to

one active body per identity. The earlier review of a one-identity/multiple-body
draft is superseded by this record.

## Independent reviews

Kimi Code CLI, dispatched through ia-bridge `/build`, first returned **READY
WITH NOTES** against issue #7. It confirmed all seven acceptance criteria and
identified four concrete cleanup items: the missing membership domains in the
DM-011 registry, an open genesis signer shape, imprecise possession-preimage
wording, and the stale review record. All four were corrected.

An independent ontology/protocol review then returned **NOT READY** while the
following interoperability gaps remained:

1. replacement-governance possession used the authorization preimage rather
   than the distinct DM-011 possession preimage;
2. prose and scenarios disagreed between every replacement signer and a mere
   threshold;
3. signer evidence did not have one closed, deterministic schema;
4. a direct reply required an exact `me_id` but did not bind it in the signed
   event;
5. `/we.sync` lacked canonical cursors, directed-leg IDs, and terminal receipt
   semantics;
6. membership fork detection did not explicitly retain occupied-position
   commitments below the current high-water.

The publication candidate resolves those findings by separating detached
authorization endorsements from a deterministic complete signer-evidence
body; requiring possession from every replacement governance signer over the
DM-011 possession preimage; signing `direct_recipient_me_id`; freezing a V0
sync attempt/cursor/leg/receipt contract; and retaining per-position membership
commitments so late sibling evidence quarantines the chain.

Additional adversarial hardening added explicit resource ceilings, fail-closed
fork recovery, auditable alias repinning, and authorization before membership
or cursor disclosure.
