## Adversarial findings resolved

The direct pass found and corrected these material ambiguities before the
candidate was marked ready:

- **human-key verifiability:** the first human wrapper named only a derived
  contact ID, leaving no public descriptor from which to verify it; the exact
  Ed25519 descriptor is now signed inside the body and MUST recompute the
  contact ID;
- **recipient-key substitution:** cards initially carried only an encryption
  key ID; they now carry the full X25519 descriptor, which a Daimon card MUST
  byte-match to the cited accepted certificate;
- **grant/key lifetime coupling:** grants now bind a Daimon subject's stable
  genesis ID/hash, not its short-lived operational or encryption key; current
  cards select delivery keys without silently retargeting authority;
- **newborn relationship collision:** the initial birth context omitted the
  nonce used for its derived relationship; the signed grant now carries that
  fresh nonce and deterministically binds the root resource controller and
  newborn;
- **parent impersonation:** a delegating parent could have appeared to become
  the tribe resource authority; the derived relationship now attributes the
  parent as grantor while retaining the exact root controller from the complete
  grant ancestry;
- **multi-authority laundering:** one child grant could have combined unrelated
  authority; every grant now has exactly one parent, and all newborn
  permissions in one grant must reach the same root controller;
- **delegation/birth races:** each parent grant has a predecessor-linked child
  lane; gaps or siblings fail closed, birth allocations count exact acceptance
  IDs, and revocation never refunds budget; and
- **transport promotion:** cards exchange signed route references, capabilities,
  and recipient keys, but protected endpoint resolution stays downstream and
  directories/adapters cannot create or expand relationships, grants, or
  recipient sets.

## Normative design

- **Bilateral membership:** exact offer plus exact responder acceptance creates
  a relationship. Cards, proposals, routes, and directories are insufficient.
- **Human boundary:** a self-certifying human-contact key supports consent and
  grants without claiming legal identity or inventing a `/me`.
- **Directional grants:** membership is symmetric; authority is directional.
  Grants require exact subject acceptance and one complete controller chain.
- **Indivisible resources:** resource IDs are content-derived. V0 has no path,
  wildcard, alias, or semantic-containment expansion.
- **Mechanical attenuation:** child permissions are exact parent subsets;
  delegation, depth, birth count, start, expiry, controller, classification,
  and policy can only remain equal or narrow.
- **Fail-closed lifecycle:** closure, expiry, revocation, relinquishment,
  compromise cutoffs, missing ancestry, or forks deactivate descendants without
  deleting canonical evidence.
- **Fresh newborn authority:** the parent issues a new grant only after the
  independently generated newborn identity exists. The newborn accepts it;
  parent keys, grants, sessions, routes, caches, and credentials never copy.
- **Observer-relative freshness:** non-revocation is stated only at an exact
  evidence cursor. Sensitive use can require fresh/online controller evidence.
- **Remote knowledge:** a knowledge grant permits attributed access. A bounded
  delivery cache remains disposable external state, never autobiography or
  silent inherited memory.
