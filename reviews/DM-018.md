## Normative design

- **One generic narrow waist:** every provider advertises an exact manifest and
  consumes content references through closed invocations/results. No path,
  database, raw secret or implementation handle becomes a protocol.
- **Authority denial:** all adapter manifests fix five Matrix authority flags
  to false. Provider success remains evidence pending Matrix validation.
- **Exact negotiation:** local ordered preference intersects explicit exact
  versions. Unknown fields/versions fail before effects; no lexical “latest”,
  implicit minor compatibility, guessing, or silent downgrade exists.
- **Immutable migrations:** export/plan/apply/verify/commit produces a
  predecessor-linked receipt. Rollback is a newer compensating receipt and all
  identity/event/memory/presence/witness/deployment/migration high-waters remain
  monotonic.
- **Species boundary:** capability runners receive exact DM-014 content
  manifests and return evidence-only measured outputs with a null application
  receipt. Matrix alone validates compatibility and application.
- **Body profile:** body realization, activation, park, wake and deployment
  fence are separately named, content-bound exchanges. Provisioning creates a
  body, never `/me`, `/we`, species membership, or autobiography.
- **Independent gates:** `daimon-presence-lease/v0` and
  `daimon-deployment-fence/v0` have different issuers, IDs, sequences and
  authority. When deployment control is configured, delivery/ACK/effect needs
  both current heads. The corpus recomputes a real fence ID and verifies its
  deterministic Ed25519 signature under the new domain.
- **Failure consumes position:** expiry, release, failed wake, restore,
  rollback and GC cannot reset or reuse lease/fence positions. A source can be
  reactivated only through strict successors.
- **Three continuity cases:** same-identity relocation, distinct-identity
  seeding, and multi-member `/we` operation have separate executable fixtures.

## Adversarial findings resolved during implementation

- **DM-011 corpus collision:** isolated the new fixtures rather than weakening
  byte-identical regeneration.
- **presence/fence substitution:** a presence-shaped object fails the
  deployment-fence schema; the dual-gate checker refuses a missing fence.
- **shape-only fence evidence:** the accepted fence and monotonic-regression
  candidate now have recomputed content IDs, a dedicated synthetic deployment
  key, and valid domain-separated Ed25519 signatures; regression is therefore
  the isolated rejection reason.
- **rollback regression:** negative migration and deployment fixtures lower
  several high-waters and are rejected even though each record is structurally
  valid.
- **adapter authority escalation:** schemas reject a capability runner that
  claims Matrix authority, and its positive exchange is evidence-only.
- **secret/implementation leakage:** activation records are closed and reject
  a `private_key` field; the normative contract also forbids paths, databases,
  sockets, tokens and session dumps.
- **identity collapse:** relocation requires the same `me_id` with one active
  body; seeding requires distinct IDs and empty personal lanes; `/we` requires
  one independent presence/fence pair per distinct member.
- **version ambiguity:** manifests accept only exact V0 rows and tests show
  deterministic caller-preference selection rather than provider-driven
  downgrade.
