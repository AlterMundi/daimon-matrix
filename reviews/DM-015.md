## Adversarial findings resolved

The direct review found and corrected these material ambiguities before the
candidate was marked ready:

- the first cursor sketch mixed inline rows with paged rows, making the signed
  shape ambiguous; it now uses closed, content-addressed, null-terminated page
  chains with exact counts and a logical snapshot hash;
- claim/import decisions named only an opaque evidence hash, preventing
  portable reproduction; both now cite a closed immutable evidence snapshot
  containing every policy input and observed cursor;
- the first tombstone rule required a currently asserted claim, which could
  make withdrawal impossible after source retraction; tombstones may now cite
  the exact historical claim that authorized the preceding publication while
  new publication still requires a current claim;
- `source_core_hash` initially risked diverging from the domain-separated
  digest inside `source_id`; the selector now requires that exact suffix;
- a retraction could appear to alter only selected relations; it now copies and
  retracts the complete predecessor relation set;
- publisher-declared `consent = explicit` could be mistaken for proof of
  consent; disclosure and promotion require the evidence demanded by exact
  local policy; and
- `tribe-shared` could be mistaken for source-derived disclosure authority; it
  now also requires an independent current relationship grant.

## Normative design

- **Self-claim only:** a claim author and claimant are the same accepted
  `me_id`. A signature authenticates the assertion, not ancestry truth.
- **Stable source:** `source_id` derives from an exact closed source core.
  Aliases, URI normalization, names, embeddings, indexes, and routes never
  merge sources or grant membership.
- **Local admission:** every valid assertion begins quarantined. A
  receiver-authored assessment over exact policy and evidence admits, keeps,
  or rejects the exact current head. Remote assessments are evidence only.
- **Fork/replay safety:** claim, assessment, publication, and import-decision
  series are ordered and retain occupied positions. Siblings quarantine; no
  arrival, timestamp, hash, popularity, or evidence-count rule selects one.
- **Provenance:** publications bind stable source URI, publisher, source claim,
  content, authorship strength, full derivation DAG, consent, classification,
  license, revisions, and tombstones. Derived output never erases original
  authors or turns a model/indexer/receiver into the author.
- **Safe intake:** diff and incoming are read-only and content-bound. Pull is
  receiver-local, resumable, and idempotent; it imports canonical evidence and
  emits an initial quarantine decision, never auto-promotion.
- **Separate promotion:** promotion is a later signed local decision over
  immutable policy, complete evidence, current claim/publication heads, final
  rendered bytes, and a DM-017 category. External content can never become
  autobiographical/body experience.
- **Adapter boundary:** HMK and collective-memory remain outbound/inbound
  adapters and projections. No SQLite copy, corpus path, Atlas node, index row,
  or adapter receipt becomes source authority.
- **Abuse resistance:** oracle-resistant denials, bounded pagination/graphs,
  no ambient credentials, SSRF denial, inert content, archive/decompression
  bounds, and no executable preview are normative.

## Cross-spec amendments

- DM-011 registers the five DM-015 event types and its content/series/hash
  domain-separation labels.
- DM-012 requires an exact source selector, locally assessed current claims,
  DM-015 snapshot cursors, mutation-free incoming, and quarantine-first pull.
- DM-013 source references are exact source-claim event IDs authored by the
  parent and current when the offer is issued; their validity remains
  contextual and never affects newborn identity validity.
- ONTOLOGY and PLAN now point to this normative contract without turning
  `/source` into identity, `/we`, species, tribe, disclosure, or memory
  authority.
