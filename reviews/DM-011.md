Date: 2026-08-01

DM-011 received two independent read-only review lanes: Kimi Code CLI through
ia-bridge `/build`, and a focused ontology/state-machine review. Neither
reviewer edited the candidate.

## Disposition

**READY.** The final candidate contains 205 deterministic vector files and 189
indexed scenarios: 171 executable and 18 explicitly documented. The index-led
suite passes 21/21 tests, including byte-identical regeneration, oracle
sensitivity, RFC 9180 known-answer material, cryptographic edge cases, and exact
inventory coverage. `git diff --check` and Python compilation are clean.

Kimi's first complete pass returned **NOT READY**. It found that the executable
certificate oracle contradicted DM-010 by making explicit certificate
carry-forward impossible, and that three DM-010 acceptance scenarios were not
exercised. The repaired candidate adds:

- exact-ID certificate carry-forward across ordinary root rotation;
- rejection when one operational signing key is certified under two `/me`
  identities;
- rejection when acceptance skips a validated but unaccepted certificate
  generation;
- rejection of recovery signed by transport-governance authority;
- consistent event-high-water ordering and identity-wide lease-high-water
  shape;
- half-open validity intervals for every V0 artifact.

Kimi's repair audit then returned **READY** with no remaining blocker. The
ia-bridge audit artifact is timestamped `20260801-053524`.

## Identity and presence corrections

The focused ontology review required DM-011 to preserve the corrected
three-level model: `/we` is a collective of distinct root-bearing `/me`
identities; each `/me` has one current body; operational credentials are
subordinate signing streams, not identities or collective-membership
authority.

That review drove an identity-wide lease-head receipt protocol:

- signed lease candidates remain `uncommitted` until independent receipt
  evidence is accepted;
- park/wake advances one identity-wide lease sequence across body or
  credential changes;
- a receipt binds the exact predecessor and its checkpointed event cutoff;
- a null cutoff is copied null-to-null when no checkpoint was retained;
- a same-body restart may reuse its operational credential, while a body/key
  move retires the prior credential;
- conflicting receipt-bearing successors quarantine the `/me`;
- all accepted receipts for one lease have unordered set semantics, including
  receipts that arrive after that lease became a historical head.

The last point was the final adversarial finding. The oracle now retains
receipt sets by committed lease ID, and the executable scenario verifies both
orders: an alternative receipt before the successor, and the same receipt
arriving after the successor is already current. The final focused re-review
returned **READY**.

## Residual boundary

DM-011 freezes canonical artifacts and conformance behavior; it is not the
production state-machine implementation. DM-012 owns collective membership and
scope resolution, DM-018 freezes normative body/capability descriptions,
DM-021 implements the durable ledger/state machines, and DM-073 remains the
release-gating security review.
