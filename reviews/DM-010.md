# DM-010 independent adversarial review

> Superseded semantic baseline: this review evaluated the earlier model in
> which one `/me` could have multiple simultaneous incarnations. Issue #5 was
> reopened on 2026-08-01 after the CompAII/daimon-cluster review established
> the three-level `/we` → distinct `/me` identities → one current body model.
> Its cryptographic findings remain useful, but the corrected specification
> requires a fresh independent review before DM-010 can close again.
> All section numbers and “incarnation” terms below refer to the superseded
> revision at commit `5405ec4`; they are historical pointers, not current
> normative terminology.

Date: 2026-08-01

Scope: [`specs/identity-continuity.md`](../specs/identity-continuity.md)

DM-010 received four analysis-only reviews before publication: an independent
Kimi Code CLI review dispatched through ia-bridge `/build`, plus focused
ontology, pre-Daimon reuse, and adversarial-security reviews. Reviewers did not
edit the specification or mutate Git state.

After the findings below were integrated, a second Kimi `/build` pass returned
**READY** and found no remaining release blocker. Its two optional wording
notes were also applied: recovery now installs one explicit post-recovery root
set, and only the incarnation's first-ever lease omits a predecessor.

## Kimi disposition

Kimi's first-pass verdict was **conditionally sound** and identified five
blocking ambiguities. The publication candidate resolves each one:

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| certificate validity across root rotation was ambiguous | Section 13 separates unaccepted, carried-forward, invalidated, and newly issued-by-old-root cases |
| incarnation sequence spaces were underspecified | Section 8 makes sequences domain-specific and defines fork identity; Section 10 makes the lease chain contiguous |
| replacement-key possession proofs were replayable | Section 5 binds every proof to the exact canonical artifact and domain |
| compromise cutoff lacked a verifiable unit | Sections 6.3 and 9 use signed control positions and per-incarnation high-water positions, with time only informational |
| genesis policy mutability was implicit | Section 4.1 makes genesis bounds immutable in V0 and permits stricter local policy only |

Kimi's non-blocking findings were also incorporated: checkpoint-relative
validation, recovery-quorum loss consequences, revoked-key non-reappointment,
one session identifier per process session, descendant fork quarantine,
explicit lease-gap behavior, root-set epoch definition, eventual clone
detection, algorithm-version boundaries, and the distinction between `/we`
eligibility and downstream selection.

The final cross-review added two stricter safeguards beyond Kimi's report:
threshold-valid but different genesis statements for one `me_id` are a
recoverable genesis fork, and ordinary root rotation can carry forward only
explicit certificate IDs. The latter prevents a retired root from minting a
backdated certificate and claiming it existed before rotation. The review also
made post-expiry, previously unseen events non-canonical unless a pre-expiry
checkpoint establishes their timeliness.

## Focused review disposition

- The ontology review confirmed that `me_id` excludes harness, model, host,
  body, name, Tribe membership, and species metadata; it also required an
  honest distinction between cryptographic authority and sentience.
- The reuse audit allowed Tribe v1's closed schemas, key-purpose separation,
  canonical hashes, and anti-rollback patterns, while rejecting Tribe
  governance, HMK, compaii-state, manifests, host identity, or GitHub as `/me`
  authority.
- The security review added recovery generations, fail-closed fork behavior,
  typed offline root ceremonies, restore-tested custody, explicit recovery
  consequences, bounded certificate/lease lifetimes, and negative vectors for
  rollback, replay, cloning, role confusion, and irrecoverable loss.

## Residual boundary

This review establishes a normative contract, not an implementation audit.
DM-011 must provide canonical encodings and interoperable vectors; DM-021 must
implement the state machines; DM-073 remains the release-gating independent
security review of custody and recovery behavior.
