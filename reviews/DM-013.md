## Normative design

- **Birth offer:** an ordinary DM-011 `matrix/birth-offer` event signed by an
  accepted parent operational credential. The closed payload contains no
  newborn identifier or key. Its fresh Ed25519 `awakening_key` is a one-use
  invitation capability, never identity authority.
- **Birth acceptance:** a mergeable threshold artifact under
  `daimon/birth-acceptance/v0`. The invitation capability signs the exact
  acceptance core under `daimon/birth-awakening-challenge/v0`; the newborn root
  threshold endorses the complete body, including that proof. The body binds
  parent, offer, newborn genesis, species release, source references, and future
  tribal commitments.
- **Parent receipt:** an optional `matrix/birth-receipt` event. It is lineage
  evidence, not newborn authority and not portable clock proof by itself.
- **First awakening:** an ordered fail-closed ceremony completing newborn
  custody, genesis, acceptance, generation-zero operational certificate,
  subject acceptance, body/capability provenance, the identity-wide first
  lease, and an external lease-head receipt before `active`.
- **Memory boundary:** newborn autobiographical and body-experience streams are
  mechanically empty because no newborn-credential-authored lived-experience
  event exists yet. Parent HMK, ledger, session, chat, state, and body history
  cannot be relabelled as newborn experience.
- **Membership boundary:** birth creates one self-certifying `/me`; it neither
  creates `/we` membership nor copies a parent relationship or grant.

## Findings and dispositions

### Invitation possession and portable verification

The initial draft used a secret HMAC response while forbidding disclosure of
the secret. Third-party verifiers therefore could not validate invitation
possession. The final protocol uses a fresh Ed25519 capability keypair: the
offer publishes its DM-011 public descriptor, the private key travels once over
the confidential bootstrap path, and the acceptance carries a public signature
over its exact core. Newborn root endorsements cover the entire acceptance body.

Verifier-side cross-role checks reject reuse against parent/newborn Ed25519
identity and operational descriptors and known offer capability keys. The text
also states the honest DM-011 limit: reused seed material across Ed25519 and
X25519 public keys is not wire-detectable and remains a custody requirement.

### Late evidence and backdating

The first independent gate found a contradiction: one validation rule rejected
every acceptance first observed after expiry while later sections permitted a
historically timely acceptance with portable evidence. The final rules split:

- `core.accepted_at_ms` inside the offer interval is a hard signed-content check;
- verifier-local pre-expiry durable observation establishes local timeliness;
- otherwise the complete DM-011 external-witness chain is required; and
- without either, evidence is retained as `time-unverifiable` and never
  projected or accepted.

The portable chain is exact: parent birth receipt, parent event checkpoint
covering it, a valid parent lease, and a distinct locally designated witness's
accepted lease-head receipt whose `event_cutoff` names that checkpoint and whose
accepted time is inside the offer interval. A parent timestamp, HLC, checkpoint,
or root high-water alone is explicitly insufficient.

### Resource ceilings

The first independent gate found that a valid 1048576-byte offer could contain
copied context too large for the 262144-byte acceptance wrapper. The final offer
caps the JCS encoding of its copied species/source/commitment context at 131072
bytes. Count bounds remain independently enforced and all complete wire
ceilings still apply before parsing or cryptography.

### Restart, replay, forks, and split brain

- exact replays are idempotent and endorsement subsets merge;
- a second distinct timely acceptance for one offer quarantines lineage, never
  either independently self-certifying newborn identity;
- before acceptance an aborted ceremony may discard an unfinished local genesis;
- after acceptance it must resume the same durable artifacts idempotently; and
- distinct receipt-bearing first leases for one `me_id` quarantine that identity
  as DM-010 split brain and never expand `/we`.

### Downstream dependencies

Unavailable DM-014/DM-015 species or source semantics produce
`context-incomplete`; they grant no authority and do not prevent the independent
identity from awakening. DM-016 must later issue fresh attenuated grants bound
to newborn keys. DM-017 must preserve the birth-time memory categories. DM-018
owns the already-declared body/capability schemas.
